Community Guidelines

KG CEOs & Entrepreneurs Network is intended to be a trusted, constructive space for business leaders. Members and applicants are expected to:

  • Be respectful, professional, and inclusive, even when opinions differ.
  • Protect the confidentiality of private conversations and information shared in member spaces. Get permission before quoting, recording, or forwarding another member’s contribution.
  • Share accurate information and be clear when something is an opinion, promotion, or business solicitation.
  • Ask for consent before sending promotional messages, sharing someone’s contact details, or making an introduction.
  • Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, impersonate, or deliberately mislead other members.
  • Do not post spam, unlawful material, malware, or content that infringes another person’s rights.
  • Use the report feature or contact the community team when you see a concern. Do not retaliate against someone for raising a good-faith concern.

Moderation

Administrators may review reports and take proportionate action, including removing content or restricting access. Serious or repeated breaches may result in suspension or removal from the network.
