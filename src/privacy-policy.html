Privacy Policy

KG Multisector Limited operates KG CEOs & Entrepreneurs Network. This notice explains how we handle information when you visit the site, apply for membership, or take part in the member network.

Information we collect

Applicants may provide their name, email address, phone number, location, company and role details, industry, business links, profile photo, and written application responses. Members may add profile details, a profile photo, preferences, messages, forum posts, event registrations, and subscription payment references.

When you submit a subscription reference, we collect the payer phone number and transaction reference to let an administrator check the payment. We do not request or store your Mobile Money PIN, card security code, or account password.

How we use information

  • Review membership applications and communicate decisions.
  • Create and maintain member accounts, profiles, directories, events, messages, and community features.
  • Verify subscription payments and provide membership support.
  • Protect the service, moderate content, respond to reports, and meet applicable legal requirements.

Visibility and sharing

Application details are available to authorized administrators for review. Directory profile fields and community posts are available to signed-in members according to your settings and the feature’s purpose. Payment references are restricted to you and authorized administrators. Profile and community uploads are stored in access-controlled storage; avoid posting confidential business or personal information in shared areas.

We use GitHub Pages to serve the public website and Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage. Mobile Money payments are sent directly to the number shown on the subscription page; KG Multisector administrators use the submitted reference to verify them. Do not share your passwords or payment PIN with us.

Retention and security

We retain information while it is needed to administer applications, membership, subscriptions, and community safety, and for any period required by law. Access controls and database policies are used to protect member-only information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You can update profile details and directory visibility in member settings. To ask about access, correction, deletion, or a privacy concern, contact us using the details below. We may need to retain some records where required for legitimate administration or legal compliance.
